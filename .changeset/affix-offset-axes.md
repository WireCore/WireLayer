Affix: `offsetX` (side edge, start/end) and `offsetY` (top or bottom edge) set one distance each, e.g. higher above a
banner; `offset` stays the distance from both edges and the default of the two. In HTML: `--wc-affix-offset-x` and
`--wc-affix-offset-y`.
