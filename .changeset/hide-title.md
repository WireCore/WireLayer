`DialogContent` and `DrawerContent` take `hideTitle` (Vue `hide-title`), like `PopoverContent`: the title stays the
accessible name of the dialog but is shown for screen readers only – e.g. around a video. Recipe variant `hideTitle` for
`dialog` and `drawer`.
